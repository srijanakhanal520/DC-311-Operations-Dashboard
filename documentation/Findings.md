# Key Business Findings

## DC 311 Service Operations Dashboard — 2025

The following findings summarize key operational patterns identified from the 2025 DC 311 service-request dashboard.

### 1. Highest Service Volume

Bulk Collection recorded the highest request volume with 67,414 requests, followed by Parking Enforcement with 58,149 requests. This represents a difference of 9,265 requests. The high volume indicates that Bulk Collection is an important area for workload and service-capacity planning.

### 2. Peak Monthly Demand

Service-request volume reached its highest level around July 2025 at approximately 45,000 requests, after increasing from approximately 30,000 in February. Volume generally declined during the later months of the year. This pattern can help managers anticipate periods of higher service demand.

### 3. Organization Workload

DPW managed the largest volume of completed requests among the organizations displayed in the dashboard. Its workload was substantially higher than the other organizations shown. This indicates that workload volume should be considered when evaluating organization-level performance.

### 4. Largest Backlog

Sidewalk Repair had the largest backlog among the Top 10 service categories with 726 requests, compared with 536 for Bee Treatment and Inspection and 408 for Tree Pruning. This makes Sidewalk Repair an important category for continued backlog monitoring.

### 5. Longer Resolution Time

Scheduled Yard Waste had an average resolution time of 14.2 days, compared with an overall median resolution time of 2.9 days. Bulk Collection averaged 7.2 days and Illegal Dumping averaged 6.1 days. These differences identify service categories that may warrant further review of their resolution processes.

## Interpretation Note

These findings describe patterns observed in the dashboard and should not be interpreted as evidence that an organization is understaffed or performing poorly. The dataset does not contain staffing information, and different service categories may have different operational requirements and expected completion timelines.
