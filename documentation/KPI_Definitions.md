# KPI Definitions

This document defines the key performance indicators (KPIs) used in the DC 311 Service Operations Dashboard for 2025.

## Total Requests
The distinct count of service request IDs in the dataset.

## Completed Requests
Service requests with a standardized status of Closed.

## Backlog Requests
Service requests categorized as Open or In-Progress.

## Completion Rate
The percentage of performance-eligible requests that were completed.

## Due-Date Performance Rate
The percentage of eligible completed requests that were resolved by their service due date.

## Average Resolution Days
The average number of days required to resolve completed service requests.

## Median Resolution Days
The median number of days required to resolve completed service requests.

## Completed by Due Date
Completed requests resolved on or before the assigned service due date.

## Completed After Due Date
Completed requests resolved after the assigned service due date.

## Backlog Over 30 Days
Backlog requests that have remained unresolved for more than 30 days.

## Open Requests
Service requests with a standardized status of Open.

## In-Progress Requests
Service requests with a standardized status of In-Progress.

## Excluded Requests
Canceled, duplicate, and transferred requests retained for source transparency but excluded from completion-rate calculations.

## Missing Resolution Date
Records where a resolution date is not available.
